# Cartridge and disc renders

The cartridge, card and disc images in this folder are renders of the 3D models below, all
licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), except the N64 cartridge,
which is [CC BY-NC 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/). For Jewel Case
they were rendered front-on in Blender, with their labels replaced by a blank sticker and
any logos and label art removed; the Game Boy and DS models were also recoloured. The
scripts that made them are in `tools/render/`.

| Template | Model | Author |

| `tall` (NES) | [NES Cartridge](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/nes-cartridge-e3c8de99be1740ae8fa551aa9b2596d2) | [HippoStance](https://sketchfab.com/hippostance) |
| `small`, `small_dark` (Game Boy, Game Boy Color) | [Gameboy Cartridge](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/gameboy-cartridge-7c0160c8097a43e0a5e146bf4d7e01a1) | [Karolina D](https://sketchfab.com/KarolinaArts) |
| `slim` (Game Boy Advance) | [Gameboy Advance Cartridge](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/gameboy-advance-cartridge-38c1e6702e5d4f21af1d0930689b1d10) | [Vxcl](https://sketchfab.com/vxclhd) |

The N64 model is licensed for non-commercial use only, so these renders of it may not be used
commercially either; Jewel Case is free. The templates for systems without a model here
(Genesis and others) are still drawn by
`tools/make_media.py`.
